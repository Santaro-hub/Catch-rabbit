RABBIT CHASE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Rabbit Chase is a small, single-player game. The game does not require an account and does not send your game progress to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Rabbit Chase uses your browser or app's local storage to save game progress and preferences, such as your high score, coins, inventory, and selected customizations. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to the developer. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or your browser's site data; doing so will erase saved progress.

On the website, the game files may also be cached in your browser's Cache Storage so the game can load offline after your first visit. These cached files do not contain personal information.

Information collected by the developer

The game does not include analytics, advertising, user accounts, location tracking, or a game server. The game itself does not collect or transmit personal information.

Website hosting

When you visit the website, GitHub Pages may process standard connection information (such as your IP address and browser request) to deliver and protect the site. This is handled by the hosting provider under its own policies. The game does not receive this information.

Children's privacy

Rabbit Chase does not ask players to provide personal information. If you have a question or concern about privacy, contact the developer below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The latest policy will be available at this page.
